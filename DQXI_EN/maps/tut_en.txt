1=3D/2D Mode Controls
2=While 3D is shown on the top screen and 2D on the bottom, use the Circle Pad to move in 3D on the top screen, or the +Control Pad to move in 2D on the bottom screen.
3=Talking and Searching
4=Press A to talk to people and search things. When you stand in front of something you can search, a ↓ mark appears, so use it as a guide and search all sorts of things.
5=If You Get Lost
6=If you're not sure where to go next, talk to people with pink speech bubbles. They'll give you hints such as your next destination.|In 2D mode, look for people with pink speech bubbles on the bottom-screen map.
7=Special Movement
8=Out in the field there are rocks and vines you can climb up and down, and in towns there are ladders and ropes you can use. Get close and see if you can move along them.
9=Helpful Companions
10=Sometimes companions will appear to help you on your adventure. They don't join your party, but they may travel with you, show you the way, or even fight alongside you in battle.
11=Time and Weather
12=Time passes in this world.|Depending on the time of day, townsfolk, shops and the weather may change. Some monsters only appear at certain times or in certain weather.|At inns you can rest until night or stay until morning, so try adventuring in all sorts of conditions!
13=Saving
14=Pray at a church or a Goddess statue to save your progress in an adventure log. You can then continue from that point, so make a habit of praying often.
15=Quick Save
16=Choose Quick save from Misc. on the menu to record your progress in a quick-save log and take a break.|To resume, choose Continue quick save.|There is only one quick-save slot. Once overwritten, you can't go back to the old one.
17=Gathering Spots
18=In the field you'll find sparkling spots on the ground, and trees, rocks and plants you can search for items.|Once you take an item the sparkle disappears, but after a while you can search there again.
19=3D Mode and 2D Mode
20=You can switch between 3D and 2D mode at churches and at Goddess statues in camps. The look of towns and people and the way you meet monsters differ, but the adventure is the same, so pick whichever you like.
21=Items
22=You'll find all sorts of items on your journey: tools you use, and equipment you wear.|Each person can carry only so much. Extra items go into the Item bag and Gear bag, which can't be used in battle.|Vital items are kept as Key items and can't be thrown away or sold.
23=Items
24=Choose Items on the menu to use the items you've collected. Items have all sorts of effects, and some disappear once used. Details are shown on the bottom screen, so check new items when you get them.
25=Mini Medals
26=Somebody in this world collects mini medals. You can trade them for prizes, so take them along once you've saved up a few.
27=Equipment 1
28=Equipment does nothing just sitting in your inventory, so wear it properly using Equip on the menu.|Equip lets you put on gear from the Gear bag or gear your allies are carrying.|Some gear can only be worn by certain people, and some changes how you look.
29=Equipment 2
30=Equip can also choose gear for you.|Pick a gear type to suit the character and enemy (Attack, Magic or Defence type) with Left/Right on the +Control Pad, then press X to equip the best gear for every slot except accessories.
31=Equipment 3
32=In battle, the Equip command also lets you swap in gear you're carrying or gear from the Gear bag.
33=Tactics 1
34=Choose Tactics under Misc. on the menu to set battle tactics for each party member, yourself included.|Allies with tactics set will act on their own according to those orders.|You can change tactics mid-battle too, via Tactics.
35=Tactics 2
36=Tactics 2: Follow Orders
37=Set an ally to Follow orders to choose exactly what they do in battle: which spells and abilities to use and whom to target.|Try Follow orders when you want someone to act just as you like.
38=Skill Points
39=As you gain experience and level up, you sometimes earn skill points. Spend them to learn abilities, spells and other skills to strengthen yourself and your allies.|To learn new skills, assign points on the Skill panel under Misc.
40=Skill Panel 1
41=Skill Panel
42=On the skill panel you spend skill points to learn skills. Learning a skill opens up the neighbouring panels, so more skills become available.|Think about the order as you develop your party.
43=Skill Panel 2
44=Unlocking Hidden Panels
45=Hidden panels on the skill panel unlock once you learn all four panels next to them. After that, you can learn them.
46=Skill Panel 3
47=Skill Reset
48=At churches you can reset the skill points you've assigned, one skill type at a time. It costs 20 gold per point.
49=Skill Panel 4
50=Bulk Assign
51=Bulk assign lets you spend your skill points in one go. Choose a skill type with L and R, tick the skills you want, then choose Confirm to learn all the ticked skills at once.|Hidden panels can be bulk assigned once their seal is broken.
52=Records
53=Choose Records on the menu to view a record of your adventure, such as the items you've found and monsters you've defeated. See if you can reveal every ?!
54=Full Heal
55=Choose Full heal under Misc. on the menu to use spells efficiently and fully restore everyone's HP. Mostly heal restores nearly all HP. Fallen allies are revived and ailments such as poison are cured.|MP is used first from allies on standby. If MP runs out partway, healing stops there.
56=Zoom
57=Cast Zoom to travel instantly to any town you've visited. Cast it indoors or in a cave and you'll bump your head, so be careful.
58=Riding Horses
59=In 3D mode, check a horse in the field to ride it. On horseback you move faster than on foot, so hop on whenever you find one.
60=Camps
61=Camps are bases for your long adventure. You can rest and recover as at an inn, and some camps have merchants. You can save at the Goddess statue, just like at a church, so make good use of camps when you find them.
62=Monster Mounts 1
63=Clockwork Egg Mounts
64=Some monsters leave a mount behind when defeated. Riding one lets you reach places you can't walk to, or cross damaging ground unharmed.|Clockwork egg mounts can make powerful jumps from springboards to clear big ledges.
65=Monster Mounts 2
66=Skull Rider Mounts
67=Skull rider mounts can walk freely not only on floors but on walls too. If something blocks your way, try walking over it on the wall.
68=Monster Mounts 3
69=Blue Wasp Mounts
70=Blue wasp mounts fly through the air. Ride rising air currents to fly higher and cross water and the like. To come down near the ground, ride a descending current.|In 2D mode you can fly anywhere except forests, mountains and shallows.
71=Monster Mounts 4
72=Hellcatcher Mounts
73=Ride a hellcatcher mount and press Y to charge. If a rock blocks your path, charge into it to smash it.|In 2D mode, get close to something breakable and press A to smash it.
74=Monster Mounts 5
75=Dullahan Knight Mounts
76=In 3D mode, dullahan knight mounts move faster than walking, and pressing Y lets out a neigh that reveals monsters hiding nearby.|In 2D mode, just enjoy the feeling of riding.
77=Monster Mounts 6
78=Dragon Rider Mounts
79=Dragon rider mounts let you move while floating in the air. Ride rising currents to jump over ledges and the like.
80=Vehicles 1: Ship
81=Sailing
82=Once you have a ship, you can travel across the sea. Head for a beach or pier to disembark. In 3D mode, Alis waits nearby when you go ashore; to board again, talk to Alis at the port or shore. The ship's location is shown on the world map.
83=Vehicles 2: Cetacea
84=Flying on Cetacea
85=Play the Sky flute to ride Cetacea the sky whale through the skies. Use the Circle Pad to turn and change altitude. In 3D mode, press B to land when Cetacea's shadow has a white outline. In 2D mode you can land anywhere except sea and mountains.
86=Swimming as a Fish
87=Under the sea in 3D mode, press B to dive and Y to rise as you swim. In 2D mode, ride currents and whirlpools to get around.|To start with, try swimming around all over the place.
88=Field Attacks
89=In 3D mode, a ↓ mark appears when you get near a monster in the field. Press A then to attack it directly.|If your attack lands, you start the battle at an advantage, so go for it!
90=Party Order 1
91=Changing Your Battle Party
92=Up to four people can take part in battle; they're called the battle party.|Allies not in battle are on standby. Use Party order under Misc. on the menu to change who fights.|Try different combinations depending on the monsters and your allies' condition.
93=Party Order 2
94=Swapping Mid-Battle
95=You can swap battle party members mid-battle with the Swap command.|If the whole battle party falls, allies on standby will step in and keep fighting.
96=Wiped Out
97=If your whole party falls, you lose half your gold and return to where you last prayed. You can deposit gold at gold banks around the world; banked gold isn't lost when you're wiped out, so bank as much as you can.
98=Getting Pepped Up
99=In battle, taking hits and other conditions can get you pepped up, a state of heightened focus. Pepped-up members' names glow blue on the bottom screen and their abilities rise for a while.|The pep wears off after a few actions, but until then it carries over into your next battle.
100=Pep Powers
101=When one or more people are pepped up, they can unleash powerful Pep Powers. With Pep Powers needing several people, everyone's pep is used up afterwards. Learning abilities can unlock more Pep Powers, so check them under Stats on the menu.
102=Fun-Size Forge 1
103=The Fun-Size Forge lets you create new equipment from materials, based on recipes you've found. Recipes are found in chests and bookcases; materials come from gathering spots and sparkles, so search them whenever you see them. Some gear can only be obtained by forging.
104=Fun-Size Forge 2
105=Use Strike or an ability to hit the metal and the orange gauge grows. Aim to get it into the green success zone.|Each action uses focus and cools the metal. Once it's completely cold, you can't strike it any more.
106=Fun-Size Forge 3
107=The hotter the metal, the more force the same action applies. Use abilities to adjust the heat or strike several squares at once, and save focus as you work.|Use Look closer to examine the metal in detail. Get every gauge into its success zone and choose Finish, and you may make a fine piece with a "+" in its name.
108=Fun-Size Forge: Reforging
109=Fun-Size Forge 4: Reforging
110=Choose Reforge to try improving the quality of gear you own. This uses some reforge orbs, depending on the gear.|If you fail, the gear isn't lost. Succeed and it gets more "+" than before.
111=Poker 1
112=How to Play Poker
113=Swap cards to make a winning hand! Choose which of your five cards to Hold and which to Change, then choose Deal to swap the Change cards once.
114=Poker 2
115=Poker: Double Up
116=Make a hand and you can try Double Up. Pick a card from four that beats the card on the left. Win and your coins double; lose and you forfeit them. Know when to stop and become a poker master!
117=Poker 3
118=Poker: Treasure Chest Chance
119=While playing Double Up, a chest with several locks sometimes appears. Each Double Up win breaks one lock. Break them all and the chest opens to reveal treasure! If you fail, the chest stays as long as you keep playing, so keep at it until it opens!
120=Roulette 1
121=How to Play Roulette
122=Guess where the ball will land and place your bets! You can bet on several spots, and payouts vary by where you bet. When you're ready, press X to start! Guess right and you win coins. Trust your instincts and become a roulette master!
123=Roulette 2
124=Roulette: Jackpot
125=If the ball lands in the pocket of a number you bet on alone and that pocket stops on the JACKPOT mark, it's a jackpot! You win loads of coins!
126=Slots 1
127=How to Play Slots
128=Line up three or more of the same symbol on a line you've bet on to win! Change your bet with Up/Down on the +Control Pad.|You win coins depending on the symbols and how many line up. Several lines can win at once.
129=Slots 2
130=Slots: Metal Spin and Free Spin
131=Line up three or more liquid metal slimes or dancing jewels for a chance at loads of coins! Your winnings may double, or you may get free spins.|Make the most of your chances and become a slots master!
132=Horse Racing 1
133=Outrun three rivals and aim for first place! Win a race you've paid to enter and you get a prize based on its rank.|Watch the course carefully and dodge the obstacles to win!
134=Horse Racing 2
135=Horse Racing 2: Dash
136=Press Y with a full stamina gauge to dash, using up stamina. Touch a Seed of agility on the course to dash without using stamina. If you're too fast, brake with Down on the +Control Pad or Circle Pad.
137=Casino
138=You need tokens to play at the casino. Buy them at the token counter for 20 gold each.|Trade tokens you win for prizes at the exchange, including deluxe prizes you can't get anywhere else!
139=Tockles
140=Tockles are mysterious beings only you can sense. You'll see them all over the world. Talk to them and they'll come to live in Tickington, a village somewhere outside this world. You can also receive Tockles from other adventurers via StreetPass.
141=Tickington
142=Up to 50 Tockles can live in Tickington, and you can talk to the ones you've collected.|There's a church, an inn and other facilities, plus a forge, the Labyrinth of Time where adventure passwords lie, and the Altar Room, a gateway to strange worlds.|At the guild in Tickington's tavern you can register, stop or change StreetPass.
143=Viewing Your Tockles
144=Visit the guild in Tickington's tavern, or choose Tockles under Misc., to see the Tockles you've collected.|You can check details of the Tockles living in the village, and choose Tockles to add to your VIP album or say goodbye to.
145=VIP Album
146=Up to 20 Tockles can be added to the VIP album. You can view their profiles at any time.|If they vanish after losing a battle in the Labyrinth of Time, you can call them back by paying gold based on their rank. You can't call the same one twice, though.
147=Labyrinth of Time 1
148=Labyrinth of Time
149=Choose up to 8 Tockles from the village to take on a special dungeon, and fight your way to deeper floors.|Register at a checkpoint to pause and return to the village; you can resume there later. Returning restores the Tockles' morale, so aim for a checkpoint first.|Registering a new checkpoint overwrites the old one.
150=Labyrinth of Time 2
151=Labyrinth Battles: Wipeouts
152=If you're wiped out, as long as Tockles remain in the village you can summon up to 8 at that spot and continue.|Even if no Tockles are left, as you gain new villagers you can retry from a checkpoint you've passed.
153=Labyrinth of Time 3
154=Labyrinth Battles: Basics
155=In battle, taking damage lowers your Tockles' morale. Bring a monster's HP to zero to win. Tockles received from other adventurers have more pride the higher their rank, and will start easy labyrinths with low morale, so be careful.
156=Labyrinth of Time 4
157=Labyrinth Battles: Spells
158=Winning battles fills the skill gauge at the top right; when full, you can cast spells. Tockles' spells depend on their colour, and more of the same colour boosts the spell's power. Against monsters with barriers, spells from Tockles matching the barrier's colour work best.
159=Adventure Passwords
160=Items found in the Labyrinth of Time include things useful on your adventure, Tockle hats, and adventure passwords that only work in Tickington.|Offer a password at the Altar Room at the back of the village to visit a strange world you may have seen somewhere before, and take on all sorts of quests yourself.
161=Quests in Strange Worlds
162=In the strange worlds you visit, you'll be asked to solve all sorts of problems.|Sometimes you'll battle boss monsters empowered by a great enemy threatening that world. Defeat them and you might get something useful for your adventure!

169=StreetPass 3
